What am I looking at?

A village of rice fields through one Boro season. The truth is the simulator's water level. The belief is what KGML-Paddy says each evening using only what it has seen so far: Sentinel-2 passes, farmer app logs and the weather.

X-ray splits each field: truth on the left, belief on the right. Stripes mean the model is unsure whether the field is ponded.

Markers: a satellite pass, a wrong pass, ! a pass the model doubts (N3), "irrigate by tomorrow" alert (filled if right), irrigation today (hollow if the farmer did not log it).
